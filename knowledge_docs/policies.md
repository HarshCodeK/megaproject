## Refund Policy

All digital purchases are refundable within 14 days of the charge date.
Subscription charges are refundable only if the subscription was never used
after billing. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within
5-7 business days.

## Subscription Billing

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same calendar day each month.
If a charge appears twice for the same period, the duplicate is refunded
automatically within 48 hours of detection. Pause a subscription from
Account > Billing at any time; no charges occur while paused.

## Late Fees

Invoice payments more than 10 days overdue incur a 2% late fee, capped at
$25 per invoice. Late fees are waived on request for first-time occurrences.
