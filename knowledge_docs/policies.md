# Working policies

## Refunds

All digital purchases are refundable within 14 days of the charge date. To be
eligible, the customer must not have exceeded the free tier allowance. Refunds
are issued to the original payment method and may take 5 to 10 business days to
appear.

## Support response times

Tier 1 tickets receive a first response within 4 business hours. Tier 2 tickets
receive a first response within 1 business day. Severity 1 incidents, meaning a
full outage, are acknowledged within 15 minutes.

## Data retention

Application logs are retained for 30 days. Conversation transcripts are retained
for 90 days. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days and cover backups
scheduled for rotation within that window.

## Deployments

Production deploys require review from one engineer who is not the author. Roll
backs are a single command and take under five minutes. Feature flags are
removed within 14 days of a full release.
